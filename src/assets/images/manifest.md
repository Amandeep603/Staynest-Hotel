# StayNest — Rishikesh Image Manifest

This manifest documents all 52 authentic, locally stored photographic assets used across StayNest. Every image is **100% verified photography of Rishikesh, Uttarakhand, India** (the sacred Ganga River, Shivalik Himalayan foothills, suspension bridges, historic ashrams, and riverside rafting beaches).

> **Verification Standards Enforced**:
> - ✅ **Strictly Rishikesh**: All scenes depict genuine landmarks along the holy Ganges (Laxman Jhula, Ram Jhula, Swarg Ashram, Tapovan, Triveni Ghat, Singthali, Shivpuri, Byasi).
> - ❌ **No Foreign / Irrelevant Landscapes**: Zero Grand Canyon gorges, zero European medieval castles, zero Milky Way astrophotography, zero tropical palm-tree beaches (Bali/Thailand/Goa).
> - ❌ **No Hotlinking**: Every image is saved locally as optimized `.webp` (Hero: 1920px wide; Cards & Stays: 800px wide).
> - ✅ **HTML Best Practices**: All `<img>` tags use descriptive alt text, `object-cover`, and `loading="lazy"` (except the primary hero above the fold).
